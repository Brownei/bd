# Surface brief — index (single-page site)

Scope: single page, mode **Experience**. Audience: Angel on her birthday. Job: make her feel celebrated; action: keep scrolling, watch the reel. Content: user-pinned flow (preloader counter → collage → name → year reel → video → epilogue → signature). Constraints: placeholders honest + swappable; single page; fast load.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The page is a recovered personal broadcast tape — not a website about her memories, but the tape of them, found, spliced, and playing on a screen that still remembers CRT. It refuses the greeting-card arrangement: no pastel balloons, no floating hearts, no confetti emoji. Emotion arrives as evidence: timecodes, frame numbers, a record light that never stops blinking.

OWN-WORLD: Pitch-black tape haze (#08070a) for 100% of ground; warm fluorescent warm-white ink (#f2ece1); signal-amber (#ffb352) as the single accent (record red #e2402c and chroma cyan #57d6c8 exist only as VHS artifacts: ghosting, states, REC dot). Component language: video shells with real player chrome, stamped frame cards (rounded 10–12px, soft offset shadow), OSD strips in VT323, hairline timecode rails. Instrument Serif carries the human voice; Instrument Sans the body; VT323 all measurement.

STORY: Angel understands this is a film of her life assembled by someone who loves her; she scrolls like scrubbing a tape, years counting beside the frames, and finishes with the sender's message. The signature interaction: scroll = tape transport — a rolling year counter in a fixed rail, a live timecode derived from scroll progress, and a hairline that fills like tape passing a head.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Black preloader filling the screen: huge VT323 "000%" count pinned to the bottom edge with a full-width amber progress hairline under it, small "HAPPY BIRTHDAY" serif italic center, REC ● + TRACK-00 OSD top corners. On complete the counter bars wipe and the hero shows: six stamped frame-cards plastered and drifting across the top half, ANGEL in huge serif with chromatic ghosting across the lower half, one OSD line beneath. No action button — the scroll is the action.

FORM: code-led (engine unavailable, no image generation). Seed key: n/a (degraded run).

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance.
